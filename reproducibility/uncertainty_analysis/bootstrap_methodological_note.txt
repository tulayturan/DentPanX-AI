Confidence intervals were estimated using a nonparametric cluster bootstrap in which independent test images, rather than individual bounding boxes, were sampled with replacement. This approach preserves the within-image dependence among multiple dental findings. The intervals quantify sampling uncertainty within the internal independent test partition and do not constitute evidence of external clinical generalizability. Metrics were calculated at the confidence threshold selected exclusively on the validation partition and at a class-aware matching IoU threshold of 0.50.
