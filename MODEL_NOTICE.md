DentPanX-AI uses a trained RT-DETR-L checkpoint developed for research on the MOPG-7 panoramic radiograph dataset.

The deployment checkpoint is distributed as part of the versioned reproducibility materials for the associated research software. Its inclusion does not constitute a legal determination that trained model weights automatically inherit a particular license from the source dataset.

Reuse of the checkpoint must comply with all applicable terms associated with:

1. the DentPanX-AI software release;
2. the Ultralytics / RT-DETR implementation and any pretrained weights used during model development; and
3. the source training dataset and its applicable license or repository terms.

The checkpoint is supplied for research reproducibility. DentPanX-AI is not presented as a clinically validated diagnostic medical device, and the checkpoint should not be interpreted as independently establishing clinical validity or external generalizability.
